Privacy

The app

Streamlet runs on your Mac. It shows the web player of a streaming service in a window, like a browser does, and adds controls in the menu bar, at the notch and in a miniplayer.

  • Nothing that you play, search or do in Streamlet is sent to me or to a server of mine.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash reports and no update checks.
  • You sign in to a service in Streamlet’s window, as in a browser. Cookies and website data stay in Streamlet’s WebKit data store on your Mac. To delete them, right-click the menu bar item and choose Clear Website Data…
  • Your settings and the last service you opened are stored on your Mac.

The streaming services

When you open a service, its web player loads from that service, as it does in Safari. The service then gets the same data as in a browser, for example your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. Streamlet reads the title, the artist, the cover and the playback state from the page, so that the controls can show them. This stays on your Mac.

Besides the web player itself, the app connects to the internet in two cases:

  • It loads the cover and the icon of the service that is open, from the addresses that the service’s page gives.
  • For the suggestions in the service chooser, it loads the icons of these services from their websites.

This website
